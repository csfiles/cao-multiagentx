# SupportMesh Warranty Policy

## Overview

SupportMesh stands behind the quality of all products sold on our platform. We offer warranty coverage on eligible items to protect customers against manufacturing defects.

## Standard Warranty Coverage

All electronics and appliances sold by SupportMesh carry a **12-month limited warranty** from the date of delivery unless otherwise stated in the product description.

Warranty coverage includes:
- Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship
- Failure of components under normal use conditions
- Factory-installed software defects

## Extended Warranty by Customer Tier

- **Standard tier**: Manufacturer warranty only (typically 12 months)
- **Premium tier**: 12-month SupportMesh extended warranty on top of manufacturer warranty
- **Enterprise tier**: 24-month SupportMesh extended warranty on top of manufacturer warranty

## Product Category Warranty Periods

| Category | Base Warranty |
|----------|---------------|
| Electronics (phones, laptops, tablets) | 12 months |
| Large appliances | 24 months |
| Small appliances | 12 months |
| Furniture | 24 months (structural), 12 months (fabric/foam) |
| Accessories and cables | 6 months |
| Kitchen and cookware | 12 months |

## What Warranty Does NOT Cover

Warranty does not apply to:
- Physical damage caused by the customer (drops, spills, misuse)
- Normal wear and tear
- Consumable parts (batteries after 6 months, filters, bulbs)
- Damage from unauthorised repairs or modifications
- Cosmetic damage that does not affect function
- Products with removed or altered serial numbers

## Warranty Claim Process

1. Contact customer support and provide your order ID and a description of the defect
2. Our team will assess the claim and may request photos or a brief description
3. If eligible, we will arrange a replacement, repair, or refund
4. Shipping for warranty claims is always free

## Electronics — Specific Warranty Notes

For electronics purchased through SupportMesh:
- Screen defects (dead pixels exceeding 3 pixels) are covered within 30 days of delivery
- Battery capacity below 80% within the warranty period is considered a defect
- Firmware updates that cause device malfunction are covered

## Manufacturer Warranties

Some products are backed by the original manufacturer warranty. In those cases, SupportMesh will facilitate the claim with the manufacturer on your behalf. Enterprise tier customers receive dedicated support for manufacturer warranty escalations.

## Out-of-Warranty Repairs

For items no longer under warranty, SupportMesh can connect customers with authorised repair centres. Repair costs are the customer's responsibility but discounts may apply for Premium and Enterprise tier customers.

## Warranty for Refurbished Items

Refurbished products carry a 90-day SupportMesh warranty covering the same defects as new products.
