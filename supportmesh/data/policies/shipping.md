# SupportMesh Shipping Policy

## Overview

SupportMesh partners with major carriers to offer reliable, trackable shipping for all orders. Shipping options and timelines vary by product category, customer tier, and destination.

## Standard Shipping

Standard shipping is available to all customers at no additional cost for orders over $50.

- **Estimated delivery**: 5–7 business days
- **Carrier**: USPS, UPS Ground, or FedEx Ground (carrier assigned automatically)
- **Tracking**: Provided via email once the order ships

## Expedited Shipping

- **Estimated delivery**: 2–3 business days
- **Cost**: $9.99 flat rate (free for Premium and Enterprise tier)
- **Cutoff time**: Orders placed by 2:00 PM local time ship same day

## Overnight / Next-Day Shipping

- **Estimated delivery**: Next business day by 8:00 PM
- **Cost**: $24.99 flat rate (free for Enterprise tier)
- **Cutoff time**: Orders placed by 12:00 PM noon local time
- **Availability**: Domestic US only, excluding weekends and holidays

## Shipping by Customer Tier

| Tier | Free Threshold | Expedited | Overnight |
|------|----------------|-----------|-----------|
| Standard | $50+ | $9.99 | $24.99 |
| Premium | $0 (always free) | Free | $14.99 |
| Enterprise | $0 (always free) | Free | Free |

## International Shipping

International shipping is available to select countries. Estimated delivery is 7–21 business days depending on destination. Customers are responsible for any import duties or taxes.

## Order Processing Time

- In-stock items: Same business day if ordered by 3:00 PM
- Pre-order / backordered items: Shipped when stock is available
- Custom or personalised items: 3–5 business days before shipment

## Tracking Your Order

Tracking information is emailed once your order ships. You can also:
1. Log into your account and view "Order History"
2. Contact support with your order ID to get the latest tracking status

## Shipping Delays

Delays may occur due to:
- Weather conditions or natural disasters
- Carrier delays during peak seasons (holidays)
- Customs processing for international orders
- Address verification issues

If your order is delayed beyond the estimated window, contact customer support and we will investigate with the carrier on your behalf.

## Lost or Stolen Packages

If a package is marked as delivered but not received:
1. Wait 2 business days — packages are sometimes pre-marked
2. Check with neighbours and building management
3. Contact support — we will open a carrier investigation
4. If the package is confirmed lost, a replacement or full refund will be issued

## Address Changes

Address changes must be requested before the order ships. Once shipped, address changes may require carrier redirection fees.
